🛕 Tirupati Help Guide

Darshan • Bus • Free Nivasam

1. Darshan Directions

Rs.300 Special Entry

Book on ttdevasthanams.ap.gov.in. Reach Vaikuntam Queue Complex 1 hour before slot. Carry Aadhaar + Ticket.

📍 Directions

Free Sarva Darshan

No ticket needed. Go to Vaikuntam Queue Complex. Waiting 12-20 hours. Aadhaar compulsory.

Divya Darshan - Walk

Walk Alipiri Steps (3550 steps). Get FREE Divya Darshan token. Darshan in 4-6 hours.

📍 Alipiri Steps

Senior Citizen - FREE

Age 65+. Timing 10 AM to 3 PM. Entry Supatham Gate. Carry Age Proof + Aadhaar.

📍 Supatham Gate

2. Bus Directions

🚌 Bus Stand to Tirumala

Bus every 2 mins, 24 hours. Ticket Rs.110. Journey 45 mins.

📍 Bus Stand to Tirumala

🚂 Station to Tirumala

Direct bus from station exit. Every 5 mins. Ticket Rs.110.

📍 Station to Tirumala

🆓 FREE Bus in Tirumala

Bus Stand to Temple, Temple to Annadanam, Temple to Kalyanakatta - All FREE. Every 5 mins.

3. Free Nivasam

🆓 FREE - CRO Office Tirumala

Free hall for 24 hours. Go to CRO with Aadhaar. First come first serve.

📍 CRO Office

🆓 FREE - Srinivasam, Tirupati

Opposite Bus Stand. Free dormitory. Aadhaar needed. Best for night arrival.

📍 Srinivasam

🆓 FREE - Vishnu Nivasam

Near Railway Station. Free hall + paid rooms. 2 mins walk from station.

📍 Vishnu Nivasam

💰 TTD Paid Rooms

Rs.100 to Rs.1000. Book 3 months before on TTD website.
