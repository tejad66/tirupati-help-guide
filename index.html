0. Directions - How To Reach Tirupati

📍 By Train - Easiest Way

Come to Tirupati Station (TPTY). It is the main station, 1 km from the bus stand. From Vizianagaram, take any train to Tirupati or Renigunta. Renigunta (RU) is just 10 km away. From both stations, buses and autos are available 24 hours to Tirumala.

📍 Get Directions to Tirupati Station

🚌 By Bus

APSRTC buses run from all major cities to Tirupati Bus Stand. From Vizianagaram, take a direct APSRTC bus to Tirupati. It takes about 12 to 14 hours. Book on apsrtconline.in.

📍 Get Directions to Tirupati Bus Stand

✈️ By Flight

Nearest airport is Tirupati Airport (TIR) at Renigunta, 15 km from Tirupati town. Flights from Hyderabad, Bengaluru, Chennai and Delhi. From airport, take a taxi (Rs.400 approx) or bus to Tirupati.

📍 Get Directions to Tirupati Airport

⛰️ Tirupati to Tirumala - Ghat Road

Tirumala temple is 22 km uphill from Tirupati. APSRTC buses run every 2 minutes from Tirupati Bus Stand and Railway Station. Ticket: Rs.110 approx. Journey: 45 minutes. Taxis charge Rs.800 to Rs.1200.

📍 Get Directions - Tirupati to Tirumala

🚶 Walking Steps - Alipiri & Srivari Mettu

Alipiri Steps: 3,550 steps, 9 km, 3 to 4 hours. Starts at Alipiri, Tirupati. Open 24 hours. Free Divya Darshan token at the top.

Srivari Mettu Steps: 2,388 steps, 2.5 km, 1.5 to 2 hours. Shorter and easier. Starts near Srinivasa Mangapuram, 15 km from Tirupati. Carry water, wear comfortable slippers.

1. Darshan Tickets

Special Entry Darshan - Rs.300

Book 3 months in advance on the official TTD website or app. You must arrive according to your selected time slot.

Free Darshan - Sarva Darshan

Waiting time can be 12 to 20 hours. Join the queue at Vaikuntam Queue Complex in Tirumala. Aadhaar card is compulsory.

Divya Darshan - For Pilgrims Who Walk

If you walk via Alipiri or Srivari Mettu, you will receive a free token. Your darshan waiting time will be much shorter.

2. Rooms & Accommodation

TTD Rooms

Book online on the TTD website. Rooms are available in Tirupati and Tirumala. Book early during weekends and festivals.

Private Hotels

Budget rooms start from Rs.800 in Tirupati. Choose hotels near Leela Mahal Circle or KT Road for easy bus access.

3. Bus Guide

Tirupati to Tirumala

4. Train Guide

Nearest Stations

Tirupati (TPTY) is the main station. Renigunta (RU) is 10 km away. Book return tickets in advance.

5. Food

Free Food - Annadanam

Free meals are provided at Tarigonda Vengamamba Annadanam Complex in Tirumala. Timings: 9 AM to 10 PM.
